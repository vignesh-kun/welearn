### E-Cell
- Definition：Entrepreneurship Cell — the student entrepreneurship organization this platform serves; the app is branded as founder-grade prep material for E-Cell members.
- Aliases：entrepreneurship cell

### Team PIN
- Definition：A shared secret (default `1408`, configurable via `TEAM_PIN` env var) used as a two-step confirmation gate for adding/removing shared content: YouTube links, founder-case prompts, PPT topics, and room deletions by non-hosts. Hosts can delete their own rooms without it.
- Aliases：team pin

### Case Hub
- Definition：The Case Studies module, organized into four sub-tabs: Case Studies (video teardowns + PPT prep), Video Zone (shared YouTube library with peer quiz gating), Founder Cases (founder-scenario prompts with peer ratings), and PPT Challenges (deck submissions rated 1–5 stars).
- Aliases：case studies hub

### Video Zone
- Definition：A shared YouTube library within the Case Hub where any member can add a link (with Team PIN); users earn points by answering every peer's quiz questions, posting three of their own, and marking items watched (+2 per correct answer, +8 per quiz post, +5 completion).
- Aliases：video zone

### Arena
- Definition：Live battle-room feature supporting three modes — Group Discussion (GD), Debate (For/Against/Moderator stances), and Pitching — with mode-specific star-rating aspects grounded in published judging rubrics, server-authoritative timers, star ratings after host ends, MVP + aspect badges, Elo deltas, and AI-generated Minutes of Meeting.
- Aliases：arena battle rooms、live arena

### Founder Quiz
- Definition：Quiz module comprising five E-Cell tracks (Investment · Growth · Ops & Legal · Product · Strategy) with per-track badges, question types including founder-mode scenarios, red-flag pitch-deck audits, micro-math drills, a Pitch Pairing exercise (3 random terms → 60s elevator pitch → missing-term detection), and a Flashcards tab of startup-context flip cards.
- Aliases：founder quiz、founder-mode quiz

### My Studio
- Definition：Personal analytics and admin surface showing a 30-day activity chart, module breakdown, peer remarks posted on your profile, and the ability to change the admin PIN.
- Aliases：studio、my studio

### Leaderboard
- Definition：Multi-metric ranking surface combining points, average Arena stars, puzzles, quiz, videos, answers, decks, and streak into a podium view.
- Aliases：leaderboard
